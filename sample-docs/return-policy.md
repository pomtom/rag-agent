# Return Policy

## Time limit
You can return most items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.
Electronics (kettles, toasters, blenders) can be returned within **14 days** if the original packaging is not damaged.

## Condition
Items must be unused, clean, and in their original packaging with all accessories and manuals.
Items that show signs of use are refunded at 50% of the price, at our discretion.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Gift cards
- Personalised or engraved items
- Kitchen knives that have been taken out of their sealed packaging
- Items bought in the clearance sale (marked "final sale")

## How to return
1. Log in to your account and open **My orders**.
2. Choose the item and click **Return**. You get a prepaid return label by email.
3. Drop the parcel at any post office within 7 days of printing the label.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within **5 business days** after we receive the item.
The original shipping cost is not refunded, unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong item.
