# Shipping

## Standard shipping
- Delivery in **3 to 5 business days**.
- Costs **4.95** per order; **free** for orders above 50.00.
- Tracking number sent by email when the parcel leaves the warehouse.

## Express shipping
- Delivery on the **next business day** when ordered before 14:00.
- Costs **12.95** per order, also for orders above 50.00.
- Not available for furniture or items heavier than 30 kg.

## Weekend and holidays
Parcels are not delivered on Sundays or public holidays. Express orders placed on Friday after 14:00 arrive on Monday.

## International shipping
We ship to all EU countries. Delivery takes 5 to 10 business days and costs 14.95.
Customs duties for countries outside the EU are paid by the customer.
