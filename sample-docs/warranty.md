# Warranty

All electrical products come with a **2-year warranty** from the date of delivery.
Premium-line products (marked "Pro") have a **5-year warranty**.

## What the warranty covers
- Manufacturing defects
- Parts that stop working under normal household use
- Free repair or replacement; if neither is possible, a full refund

## What the warranty does not cover
- Damage from dropping, water, or using the wrong voltage
- Normal wear, such as scratches, worn blades, or a discoloured kettle base
- Products used for business or commercial purposes
- Repairs done by anyone other than our service centre
- Limescale damage: descale kettles and coffee machines at least once a month

## How to make a claim
Email support@example.com with your order number, a photo or video of the problem, and a short description.
We reply within 2 business days with a prepaid label to send the product to our service centre.
